Summary

  • Stacks and FITTR do not collect personal data. They process information on your device.
  • Davevite stores the information hosts and guests enter to provide the service.
  • nuttr.co does not use cookies, analytics or advertising.

Introduction

NUTTR LLC (“NUTTR”, “we”, “us” or “our”) provides the website nuttr.co and the apps Stacks, FITTR and Davevite, including Davevite’s websites (together, the “Services”). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use and disclose information in connection with the Services.

Information we collect

Information you provide. When you contact us, we collect the information you provide, such as your email address, your message and any attachments. Davevite collects the information described in the Davevite section.

Information collected automatically. When you visit nuttr.co or use Davevite, we and our service providers may automatically collect technical information, such as IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. nuttr.co does not use cookies, analytics or advertising. The Services do not respond to Do Not Track signals.

Information from app platform providers. The platform providers that distribute our apps may provide us with information such as sales and download reports, crash reports and usage statistics. If you test a pre-release version of an app, they may also provide feedback and screenshots you choose to send and basic information about your device. If we invite you to test an app by email, we have your name and email address.

How we use information

We use information to provide, operate, maintain and improve the Services, to respond to inquiries and provide support, to protect the Services and prevent abuse, and to comply with applicable law.

How we disclose information

We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising. We may disclose information:

  • to service providers that host and operate the Services or provide email services on our behalf;
  • to other users of Davevite, as described in the Davevite section; and
  • as required by law or legal process.

Service providers that receive user data from us provide the same or equal protection of that data as stated in this Privacy Policy.

Stacks

Stacks does not collect personal data. With your permission, Stacks accesses your photos and information about them, such as the date, location and albums of each photo, and processes them on your device to provide its features.

To group similar photos and select the best photo, Stacks analyzes faces in photos on your device, such as whether a person is smiling or has their eyes open. Stacks does not use face analysis to identify individuals. Face analysis is stored on your device, is not transmitted to us or shared with third parties, and is deleted when you delete the app.

Stacks stores its analysis, its settings and a record of the photos you stack on your device. Some of this information may be included in your device backups.

FITTR

FITTR does not collect personal data. With your permission, FITTR reads and writes the following health and fitness data on your device to provide its features:

  • Data FITTR reads: heart rate; active calories; workouts; walking, running, cycling and swimming distance during workouts; sleep; heart rate variability; and resting heart rate.
  • Data FITTR writes: workouts you record, with their effort ratings and, for outdoor walks and runs, their routes.

FITTR uses your location during outdoor walks and runs to record the route. FITTR stores your plans, workouts and settings on your device, including the calories and average heart rate of workouts you record. This information may be included in your device backups. If sync is turned on, FITTR also stores your plans and workouts in your personal cloud storage account, which we do not have access to.

Davevite

Davevite is a guest list service for hosts and their guests. Davevite stores the following information on servers operated by our service providers:

  • Account information, such as a host’s name, gender selection, time zone and sign-in information.
  • Information hosts provide, such as the names, phone numbers and gender of the people they add, groups, invitation preferences and event details, including times, places and addresses.
  • Information guests provide, such as responses, poll votes, the names of additional guests, answers to hosts’ questions and, when using a link to sign up or join a game, a name, a phone number or both.
  • Avatars that hosts and guests create.
  • Game information, such as the people in each game and their answers, guesses and scores.
  • Activity records, such as when a host sent invitations or reminders, when each guest first opened their invitation, and who attended.
  • Device and app information, such as the information needed to send notifications to hosts and the app version and date a host last used the app.
  • Early access requests, including the name and phone number submitted and the time of the request.

Use and visibility. We use this information to provide Davevite, including to send notifications to hosts, to review early access requests and to contact the people who submit them. Hosts can see the information guests submit for their events and games and when each guest first opened their invitation. Co-hosts and guests may see information about other guests, such as names and responses, depending on the host’s settings. Anyone who has a guest’s link can open that guest’s page. Anyone who has an event’s group link can open the event’s group page and respond for the people listed on it. Depending on the host’s settings, the group page may show guests’ names, avatars, responses, poll votes, answers to hosts’ questions and the names of additional guests. Hosts can also share a link that shows the names and avatars of the people going to an event. Players in a game can see other players’ names, avatars, answers, guesses and scores. Notifications to hosts may include guests’ names and responses.

On the host’s device. If a host allows access to contacts, Davevite reads contact names, company names and phone numbers on the device so the host can choose people to add, and stores only the people the host selects. Davevite may make event information, including guests’ names and responses, available to search and assistant features on the host’s device.

Cookies and browser storage. Davevite’s websites use cookies and browser storage to keep hosts signed in, to recognize returning guests and to remember drafts, display choices and guests’ responses.

Retention and deletion. Davevite retains information while the host’s account is active, unless it is deleted sooner. Hosts can delete people and events in Davevite, and can delete their account in Davevite’s settings. Deleted information may remain in backup copies for a limited period, and we may retain limited account records. Deleting the app does not delete a Davevite account. Guests who want information about them changed or removed should contact the host.

Retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy and for our business records. Deleting Stacks or FITTR deletes the information the app stores on your device, except copies in your device backups, health data FITTR has written and data synced to your personal cloud storage account.

Your choices and rights

You can change or revoke the access you have given our apps, such as access to photos, health data, contacts, location or notifications, in your device’s settings. To request deletion of personal information we hold, contact us.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under applicable law to request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information. To make a request, contact us.

Third-party services

The Services may let you open or share content with third-party apps and services. Their own terms and privacy policies govern the information you provide to them.

Security

No method of transmitting or storing information is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of information.

Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any update on this page and revise the effective date. Updates are effective when posted.
