A personal project.

Why it was built

There’s a movie night or another get-together almost every week. Polls for dates, keeping the mix balanced and filling open seats one text at a time took a ton of time.

Davevite was built to take that work off the host, with an invite that’s a joy to get.

Why it won’t be released

Releasing it would mean running a server that holds other people’s contact information. That’s a responsibility NUTTR isn’t taking on for a personal project.
